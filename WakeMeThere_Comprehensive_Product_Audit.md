# WakeMeThere Comprehensive Product Audit

## Scope and evidence

This register records the owner-reported items in `to_be_implemented_in_code_immediatly.md` against the current Flutter and Android sources. Corrections that the owner authorized for that list are included in the app. Source inspection and automated checks do not establish on-device behaviour; the connected RMX2027 and an emulator were unavailable for this pass. Cold/warm startup timing, real notification drawer appearance, alarm audibility, and OEM background restrictions therefore remain device checks.
